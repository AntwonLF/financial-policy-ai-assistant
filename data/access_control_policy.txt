
FICTIONAL BANK ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Version: 1.0

Purpose:
This policy establishes security requirements
for accessing internal banking systems.

1. Employee Access
All employees must receive manager approval
before accessing internal banking systems.

2. Multi-Factor Authentication
Employees must use multi-factor authentication
(MFA) when accessing sensitive financial systems.

3. Account Termination
Employee accounts must be disabled within
24 hours of employment termination.

4. Access Reviews
Department managers must review employee
access permissions every 90 days.

5. Least Privilege
Employees may access only the systems and
information necessary for their assigned duties.

6. Security Incidents
Employees must report suspected unauthorized
access to the Security Operations team within
one hour of discovery.

7. Policy Compliance
Violations of this policy must be documented
and reviewed by the Information Security team.
